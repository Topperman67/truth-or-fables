Before you write

If you are defending an organization’s beliefs, you are a biased participant. Deep investment often makes objective evaluation difficult. Cognitive dissonance and its defenses are well documented (see Festinger et al., When Prophecy Fails).

“A man with a conviction is a hard man to change. Tell him you disagree and he turns away. Show him facts or figures and he questions your sources.”

Simply put: salvation comes through faith in Christ alone. When Sabbath-keeping—or any law-keeping—is treated as necessary to obtain or keep salvation, faith in Christ alone is displaced.
