Reader’s testimonies

Published only with the writer’s approval. A few selections from the many messages received.

Kerry Wynne

From Sunday to Sunday

“It was the reading of two of your short articles on the Sabbath at Truth or Fables that opened my eyes to the need to study the Sabbath question for myself.”

Leaving Adventism opened a chain of grace in his extended family—including a stepson who trusted Christ and later experienced a documented healing during prayer—drawing still more relatives toward Jesus.

Cherokee

Website appreciation

“I just wanted to say thank you for your website. It has really helped me a lot.” After sharing truth locally—even as leadership cast her as a threat—she used the site’s form letter to request membership removal. “There is life after the SDA church, but it takes strength and courage to live it.”

Andrew Heyn

Age 20 · Raised SDA

“After seeing your web site, I see how the teachings of EGW have perverted my mind.” Denial was short-lived. He realized he could not simply attend a quieter SDA church—the teachings had shaped biblical interpretation itself—and that salvation is not burdened by old-covenant law beyond Scripture.

Rachel Sinclair

Blue Mountains, NSW, Australia

“I finally got through the Daniel 8 prophecy as written on your web site and understand the whole lot! Praise God!” A prayer group that begged God for truth eventually left together. “I praise God for the truth that YOU share with so many!”

Patricia Carr

Years after leaving

An EGW quote blaming parents’ “wrong habits” for disqualifying children at birth made her throw the lesson book and ask God if the church was wrong. Scripture on the radio—“Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ and thou shalt be saved”—broke through. “My heart aches for the precious, sincere people in the church who truly love the Lord.”
