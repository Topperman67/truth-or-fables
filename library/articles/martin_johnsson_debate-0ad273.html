Walter Martin and William Johnsson Debate 1985

The 1985 debate between Walter Martin, author of Kingdom of the Cults, and William Johnsson, the former editor of the Adventist Review took place in 1985 on the John Ankerberg Show. Discussions covered the differences between Adventism and Evangelicalism. There are 14 video clips that cover the complete debate on YouTube.
